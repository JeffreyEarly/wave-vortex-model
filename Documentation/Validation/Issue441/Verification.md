## Diagnostic sampling and required-set failure

The original 1537-point diagnostic run restored the saved 64-mode APV bases at 129 and 257 depths, with all 162 retained nonzero radii and both endpoints. Physical diagnosis compared 513 with 1026 quadrature points, then the aligned 129/257-depth diagnostic bases. Scientific providers remained unavailable throughout.

Of the [13 record comparisons](verification/diagnostic64-regimes/comparisons.csv), eleven passed. The two exceptions are the identical zero-time cold states, `cold10-observation1` and `cold100-observation1`. Each has coefficient-relative changes of `0.534508` under quadrature refinement and `0.565437` under stored-grid refinement, exceeding the unchanged 1% gate. The [comparison helper](../../../tools/compareThermalReadinessDiagnostics.m) divides the Frobenius change of each coefficient family by that family's reference norm, with no absolute floor, then takes the maximum across `Ag_q` and `Ag_0`.

The subsequent [load-only extraction](verification/cold-diagnostic-normalization/extraction.json) confirms that `Ag_q` causes the failure. Both cold records have exactly the same [extracted values](verification/cold-diagnostic-normalization/coefficient-normalization.csv):

The extraction uses only saved diagnosis arrays and saved mode alignment, constructs no transform or scientific basis, and [completed with exit 0](verification/recovery/cold-diagnostic-normalization/result.json).
