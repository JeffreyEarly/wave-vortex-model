# T10 retained accuracy and budget windows

The completed time, product, mean, native-sampling and horizontal-bandwidth windows meet the declared field-comparison gates for the six-hour manufactured stress state. The small constant-stratification control also passes its independent nonlinear-tendency comparison and trajectory checks. Separate 65-observation startup windows resolve all four inventory budgets at both 1537 and 769 product points. Thermal refinement remains inconclusive after the 513-direction reference exceeded the 32 GiB guard. Cold M10/M100 and developed peak/zero-crossing controls complete their bounded intervals; the once-refined developed phase budgets all pass, while the cold budgets retain unresolved outcomes. The once-extended three-day companion comparison meets the declared nonlinear-activity trigger. These bounded results do not establish three-day convergence, a mature seasonal campaign or physical applicability of the large-displacement stress fixture.

This report accompanies the [main validation report](README.md), [closure audit](ClosureAudit.md) and [accepted plan](../../Architecture/Issue441ThermalReadinessPlan.md). It was prepared by reading retained CSV/JSON evidence and source on 14 September 2026, without running MATLAB. The [artifact index](accuracy/artifact-index.csv) records 311 byte-for-byte source copies, totaling 969398 bytes, with SHA-256 hashes. `sourcePath` is relative to `OceanKitRepositories/thermal-readiness-t10-evidence`; `copiedPath` is relative to this report's `accuracy/` directory. The generated index uses LF line endings. No MAT trajectories, scientific-array payloads or bulk logs are copied into the repository.

## Comparison contract

The large-grid baseline is 64×64×385, with 257 thermal directions, four MDA directions, 2057 assembly points and 1537 product points. It uses the fixed 500 km square, 4 km deep exponential-stratification case, scalar diffusivity `1e-5 m2/s`, both active endpoints, strict seasonal surface forcing and bottom drag. These accuracy windows use M100 at phase `pi/2`, an initial velocity RMS of `0.1 m/s`, a supported nonzero mean, and **no optional damping**. The declared axis is the only changed numerical choice. The full frozen overrides, tolerances, observation times and run identity are retained in each copied `run-contract.json`; each case's `integration-contract.json` records its physical inventory floors.

Initial states are normalized once on the common physical rule and transferred without independent target normalization. Initial preflight measures Q/2Q field differences before trajectory setup and blocks dependent cases on failed or unresolved initial representations. Time pairs require exact scientific-array identity. Product pairs permit changes only to the product count and its two qualification residuals; physical reconstruction, assembly, diffusion and all other stored scientific data remain fixed. Native/mean comparisons allow their expected scientific-array differences and use physical field agreement as the invariant.

At each observation, the comparison evaluates the candidate/reference difference and the reference/further-refinement difference. Independent physical-depth quadratures use 513 and 1027 points for the large-grid windows, or 65 and 131 for the small constant trajectory. Both error numerators and reference-norm denominators are refined. The complete field allowance is `absoluteFloor + relativeAllowance*referenceNorm`, using the accepted 1% field and 5% gradient allowances, dimensional floors and separate endpoints. Its effective value is one tenth of the complete allowance for every temporal comparison and for every initial comparison; other spatial observation rows use the complete allowance.

A row passes only if its error is within the effective allowance and each of the reference error, comparison-quadrature uncertainty and reference-quadrature uncertainty is at most 0.2 of that effective allowance. All ratios below use this effective allowance. These are further-refinement estimates, not extrapolated continuum solutions or proofs for arbitrary future states.
