## Original prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create a new variation of the WaveVortexModel scientific-software website icon.
Image 1 is a palette and container style reference ONLY: preserve its deep ocean navy/indigo softly shaded rounded-square background, luminous icy-cyan/teal wave colors, and warm peach/coral highlights. Completely redesign the foreground. Image 2 is a scientific SHAPE reference: Kelvin–Helmholtz shear-layer billows with fluid contours rolling over and folding back along a wavy interface. Do not copy Image 2's rainbow palette or its many arrows.
Design one elegant icon whose foreground is a continuous fluid roll-up: a bundle of 5 to 7 smoothly flowing bands travels roughly diagonally through the square, undulates, and folds around one dominant compact asymmetric Kelvin–Helmholtz billow plus a smaller partial billow. The strongest billow sits just right of center. Its vortex shape is like actual stretched material contours rolling into an oval hooked fold, with nested curved layers, elongation and a tail connecting back into the flow. Use cyan bands through the wave regions and a few coral/peach bands following the inner rolls. The warm and cool ribbons visibly share the same fluid geometry. The vortex should have an organically rolled core and a long flowing tail, not a separate pinwheel, circular donut, generic radial spiral or eye. A compact S-shaped shear interface with unequal curled ends is welcome. The open portions of the bands should still visibly have wave crests and troughs. Preserve navy gaps so the contour structure is readable.
Style: sophisticated fluid-dynamics-inspired graphic, a little more intricate than the earlier icon but carefully edited into several bold broad silhouettes plus a few finer nested contours. Smooth graceful material curves and restrained luminous shading, strong contrast and generous outer margin. The image should suggest both waves and eddies in one coherent flowing emblem. No scientific plot frame, no rainbow, no text, initials, arrows, legends, eye/eyelid symmetry, surface-breaking surf, funnels, literal hurricanes, tiny particles or many independent decorative swirls. Single square icon, straight-on; keep the entire rounded-square container visible with the same corner style as Image 1.
```
