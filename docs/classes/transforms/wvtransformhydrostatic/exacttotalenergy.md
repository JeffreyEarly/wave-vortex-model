#  exactTotalEnergy

Nonlinear total energy evaluated from physical-space fields.


---

## Discussion
Nonlinear total energy evaluated from physical-space fields.

This scalar diagnostic integrates kinetic energy and available potential energy over the domain with `volumeIntegral`. Its units are cubic meters squared per second squared when normalized per unit reference density.
